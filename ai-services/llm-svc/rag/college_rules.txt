Attendance Policy: A minimum of 85% attendance is required to be eligible for final examinations. Students with less than 85% attendance will need to provide a valid medical certificate or receive special permission from the Head of Department.

Library Rules: Books can be borrowed for a period of 14 days. A fine of 5 rupees per day will be charged for overdue books. Reference books cannot be taken out of the library.

Grading System: A grade of 'S' is awarded for scores 90-100. 'A' is for 80-89. 'B' is for 70-79. A minimum grade of 'E' is required to pass a course.
